Beyond my technical engineering studies, I strongly believe in taking an active role in societal, environmental, and inclusion initiatives. Over the past few years, I have committed my time to raising awareness around accessibility and disability, the ecological transition, and the prevention of gender-based and sexual violence.

From leading introductory French Sign Language workshops for fellow students to participating in climate frescoes and university festivals, these experiences have strengthened my empathy, pedagogy, and sense of collective responsibility.

Key Initiatives & Actions

Inclusion & Accessibility Workshop Facilitator

Disability Awareness Day — French Sign Language (LSF)

During a full-day university disability awareness event, I led interactive introductory sessions for groups of students on French Sign Language (LSF). I explained how visual-gestural communication works, shared key cultural insights about the Deaf community, and taught participants practical basics—such as fingerspelling, greetings, and how to introduce themselves.

"Futur Proche" Festival

I have participated in the annual "Futur Proche" transition festival for three consecutive years. Across these editions, I took part in collaborative frescoes and attended conferences covering the environmental impact of Artificial Intelligence, sustainable food systems, economic degrowth with an economist author, and the Matilda effect in science.

"The Trial of King Kong" — Interactive Play

I attended two performances of the interactive forum-theater play "The Trial of King Kong", addressing gender-based and sexual violence within the student community. Co-hosted by a criminal court judge, the sessions encouraged open debate on consent, bystander intervention, and legal frameworks.
